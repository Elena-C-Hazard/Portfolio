Fractals

Fractals are similar to dungeons in that they have a final boss and that you have to fight things to get to it. However, fractals have the unique aspect that they have some harder mechanics, better loot, and they scale up in difficulty levels depending on what level of fractal you are doing.

There are 25 different fractals that all have unique mechanics and achievements attached to them. They are challenging, yet fun. You form a party of up to five other players to do fractals. The lower level ones can be done with less than five party members, but as you get to higher levels of difficulty they get to the point where you have to have groups of five players that know their character's build inside and out in order to beat the fractal.

There are four levels of fractal difficulty: Initiate, which is so easy that you can do it with players that have never done fractals, and you can do it with less than five people. Adept, which is a bit harder as before in Initiate only the last six fractals begin having what is call Agony, which reduces healing and causes you to take damage every second, which can be reduced if you have the proper level of Agony Resistance infusions on your gear. Now in Adept level all of the fractals have Agony of various levels. Expert is right above Adept and has even more levels of Agony and is much harder to do, you probably should not bring inexperienced players into them. Master is the highest level on normal difficulty, has the most Agony and should be done with players that know what they are doing.

The last part of fractals is Challenge mode. Challenge mode upgrades the difficulty of fractals to the extreme. These should only be done with full groups of people who know what they are doing and have someone to apply Alacrity and Quickness to the group.

The Cliffside fractal has you start by attacking some enemies surrounding a hammer. After you defeat them you can grab the hammer and run up the ramp to the right of the alter that the hammer was on. You can only hold the hammer for a certain amount of time before you need to pass it off to another player or you will go into the downed state which is where you are almost dead but can recover, you will see a hammer symbol above your head that will start at yellow, then orange, and then red. You then have to fight some enemies that are on the next level to charge the hammer which when it is charged can be used to damage the seals. After the seal is destroyed you can run to the other side of the level, dodge some spike traps, and climb a ramp to the next level while dodging gusts of wind that will knock you off the side. After you reach the next level you can break the seal while trying to avoid puddles of fire. After which you can climb the ramp on the other side to go to the next floor where you will have to go back and fourth between two seals. After the seals are broken the hammer will disappear, at this point you can enter a hallway that is next to the ramp that you took to get up to the level that you are on, climb the ramp while avoiding explosions that will knock you off the side, and make your way to the boss room. The boss fight is pretty straight forward, every time you reduce his health by 25% until his health hits 6% he drops a hammer which you can pick up, walk to the enemy that is closest to you, kill it, and then hit the seal in the center of the room. Then you can claim the chest after the boss dies and leave the fractal.
