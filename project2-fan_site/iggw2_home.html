Welcome to Ishalana's Guide to Guild Wars 2

Guild Wars 2 is an MMORPG which stands for massively multiplayer online role-playing game. It is a game where you can do many things from gathering materials to craft a powerful legendary piece of gear that you spent months to make, gather a group of players to raid a powerful monster's lair, join a guild and do guild missions with objectives that range from running a race to doing a massive raid against other players in World vs. World, and so much more.

It takes place in the land of Tyria where there are five races that are playable: Char, Norn, Human, Asura, and Sylvari. There are more than just these five races that live in Tyria, it's just that only these five are playable.

The Char are a race of large catlike warrior people who are very crude in language and would rather fight first and ask questions later. They live on the plains and they operate like the military and work in small groups called warbands. They have horns, have a really cool run animation where they are running on all fours, and they have tails that move any skirts that are short enough that they do not clip straight through them.

The Norn are a giant race that is only eclipsed by the Char. They are a race of warriors that live in lodges in the mountains and worship spirits of the wild that are in the forms of animals. They can also transform into bipedal animals for a short time. They can have tattoos, extremely long hair, and if they are female then some of the outfits clip through them in some awkward places.

Humans are like normal people who live in the middle of Tyria. They are as diverse as we are in this world. They are smaller than both Char and Norn, but taller than Asura, they are the same height as Sylvari. They can look either Western or Eastern and they even have a face option that gives the character demonic eyes.

Asura are the smallest of the playable races, and maybe even the smallest race in Tyria. They are technologically advanced and live in a giant floating cube. They make golems that protect and help them. They have a skin tone range of gray to black, they can have markings, and they have large, floppy ears that stand up when they are in combat.

Sylvari are a race of plants. Yes, they are sentient plants that look like people. They live in the Grove which is a magical place that is inside the roots of a massive, sentient tree that is the mother of all the Sylvari. They are the same size as humans, they have leaf or bark hair and ears, are the only race that looks the closest to an elf, and they glow in the dark.

There are many things that I can dive into from Guild Wars 2 besides what I have mentioned above. However, I will make it simple by focusing on just three of them in the other pages: jumping puzzles, meta events, and fractials.
