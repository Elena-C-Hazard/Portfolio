Starbound Travelers

The planet Nepton serves as the home of the Velenar Academy. Where those who dream of exploring the unknown reaches of space go to be trained in how to survive in the wilds of unknown planets.

Take on the unknown as a cadet of the Velenar Academy. Learn how to survive in the wilds, and reach your dreams of exploring unknown planets.

But not all is as it seems. Sabotaged tests, unknown murders, and a threat to the academy itself has you on edge about if your dreams are going to be able to be reached. Or if you are going to be the next victim.
