Blizzard Games

Here at Blizzard Entertainment we can offer you three different gaming experiences with our three games:

Diablo:

Fight in an epic war between angels and demons and take on Diablo himself as you play as one of three classes.

StarCraft:

Play as three separate characters as you take part in all sides of a war set in a SciFi universe.

Warcraft:

Fight in an epic war in the land of Azeroth as you fight for either the Orcs or Humans in this fantasy game.
