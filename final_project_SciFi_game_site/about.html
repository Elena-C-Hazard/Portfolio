Meet the Creators

Elena Hazard

The programmer and storywriter of Starbound Travelers. She spend a lot of her free time playing Starbound Travelers to have fun and find any bugs that might break the game. As of today, she has found and fixed over 500 bugs in the game and continues to do so.

Isaiah Cox

The behind the scenes manager. (Also the reason for Blackholes) He created our store front, as well as the product page. He manages the media side of the website, such as video, and works on marketing strategies to help promote Starbound Travelers. He was the creator for the cover art of Expansion 1, and likes to draw pixelated blobs that he calls "Frogs" in his free time.
